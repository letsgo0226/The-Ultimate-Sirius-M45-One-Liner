# GREO: invariant high cubes and recurrence exclusion

For a=min absolute coordinate >= alpha=sqrt(2/3), all multipliers are positive. Each output magnitude is at least f(t)=t(10a²-5a⁴-t⁴)/4 where t is its input magnitude in [a,1], since b²+c²-b²c² is increasing in each argument on the unit cube. The function f is concave on [a,1], so its minimum occurs at an endpoint. f(a)=g(a), and f(1)-g(a)=(1-a)²(6a³+7a²-2a-1)/4 >=0 for a>=4/5 (lower bound the positive terms at 4/5 and negative terms by -3). Thus min_next>=g(a)>=a. The high cube is forward invariant. If a>alpha, comparison with the increasing scalar iteration g gives min_n>=g^n(a)->1, hence convergence to the signed deterministic vertex. At a=alpha, RACCO guarantees convergence to the corresponding vertex or saddle. No nonconstant periodic orbit can intersect this cube, because a convergent periodic orbit is constant. Together with GTREO and ULCO this excludes periodic orbits that intersect any of those certified regions. It does not exclude recurrence in the remaining intermediate region. Rational tests check the inequality, not the irrational boundary directly. No merge or deployment.
