# Concatenated five-qubit ideal error bound

Assume independent stochastic Pauli errors across disjoint blocks, ideal noiseless encoding, decoding and recovery. Any weight-zero or weight-one error is corrected. Logical failure is bounded by the probability of at least two failed child blocks: F(p)=10p²-20p³+15p⁴-4p⁵. F is monotone on [0,1]. The union bound F(p)<=10p² implies p_n <= (10p_0)^(2^n)/10 for p_0<1/10. This sufficient threshold is not an optimal or hardware fault-tolerance threshold. The code enumerates all 1024 Pauli labels to compute exact one-level logical failure for independent depolarizing noise; the scalar exact depolarizing formula is not assumed to hold recursively unless logical noise remains depolarizing. The upper bound remains valid under the stated independence assumptions. Concatenation requires 5^n physical data qubits plus recovery resources; finite depth cannot guarantee zero error. No perpetual computation, hardware independence or noisy-gate threshold is demonstrated. No merge or deployment.
