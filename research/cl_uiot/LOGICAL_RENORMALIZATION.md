# Exact logical channel renormalization

The full map is the four-component Pauli distribution enumerator of PEFT. On the invariant depolarizing slice, weight counts for each nonidentity logical class are (0,0,30,70,90,66). Hence with a=1-4p/3, a'=g(a)=(5a^3-3a^5)/2. This identity follows by expanding the weight enumerator; the three logical nonidentity classes have identical counts. Factor g(a)-a= -a(a²-1)(3a²-2)/2. On physical a in [-1/3,1], fixed points are a=0, sqrt(2/3),1. Thus p_c=3/4*(1-sqrt(2/3)) approximately 0.13762756. For p<p_c iterates converge to 0; for p>p_c they converge to 3/4, including p>3/4 where negative a increases toward zero. At threshold the map fixes p_c. g'(a)=15a²(1-a²)/2, equal to 0 at a=0,1 and 5/3 at the intermediate fixed point. Monotonicity and sign of g(a)-a prove these basin statements on the slice. Rational bisection brackets the threshold without floating point. This is an ideal independent Pauli concatenation threshold for a specified decoder, not a noisy hardware fault-tolerance threshold. The full anisotropic map is available but its fixed points and basins are not classified here. No merge or deployment.
