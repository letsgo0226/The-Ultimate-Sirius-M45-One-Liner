# UDLC-01 — Universal-Dimensional Life Continuity (candidate)

Status: mathematical thought experiment; no clinical or physical validation.

Let X be a state space, V an admissible subset, T_n a transition, and I a candidate invariant. If X_0 is in V, T_n maps V into V, and I(T_n(x))=I(x) on V, induction gives X_n in V and I(X_n)=I(X_0) for every finite n. This conditional result does not establish existence of indefinitely functioning physical systems or preservation of subjective identity.

For nonnegative abstract damage d and constant net loss k, define d_{n+1}=max(0,d_n+k). Then d_n=max(0,d_0+n*k); if k<0 the model reaches zero by ceil(d_0/(-k)), if k=0 damage remains unchanged, and if k>0 damage grows. A scalar is not sufficient to model biological aging, cancer, accidents or mortality.
