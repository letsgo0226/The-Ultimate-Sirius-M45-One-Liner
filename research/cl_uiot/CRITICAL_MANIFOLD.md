# Local critical manifold

Exact enumeration gives every tangent Jacobian entry on the depolarizing slice as -20p(2p-3)(4p-3)^2/81. Substitution of p_c=3/4(1-sqrt(2/3)) yields 5/9. Thus the spectrum is (5/3,0,0), with radial eigenvector (1,1,1) and transverse plane sum v_i=0. The polynomial map extends smoothly to R^3 and the critical point is interior. The local stable manifold theorem for smooth endomorphisms (hyperbolic fixed point, no eigenvalues on unit circle, invertible unstable restriction) yields a two-dimensional local stable manifold tangent to that plane. Not a global basin-boundary proof: identification of the two sides with the distant attractors requires additional nonlinear control. Zero transverse eigenvalues mean first-order suppression, not exact elimination of finite anisotropy. The polynomial coefficient audit is exact; substitution and theorem are analytical, not a numerical manifold construction. No hardware threshold, merge or deployment.
