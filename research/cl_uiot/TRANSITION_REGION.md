# GTREO: global maximum comparison and enlarged central basin

Let M=max(|x|,|y|,|z|), a=|x|, b=|y|, c=|z|. The signed multiplier numerator is P-a^4 with P=5(b²+c²-b²c²). Since b,c<=M<=1, 0<=P<=10M²-5M⁴=K. For positive output magnitude, a(P-a⁴)<=a(K-a⁴)<=M(K-M⁴), since the derivative K-5a⁴>=10M²(1-M²)>=0. For negative output magnitude, a(a⁴-P)<=M⁵<=10M³-6M⁵. Dividing by four gives M_next<=g(M)=(5M³-3M⁵)/2 globally. For 0<M<1 equality requires all three magnitudes equal: the negative bound is strict, the positive comparison increases strictly in a and P reaches K only at b=c=M. At M=alpha=sqrt(2/3), every nondiagonal point therefore enters M<alpha in one step.

For M<alpha, g(M)/M=(5M²-3M⁴)/2<1 and this ratio increases on [0,alpha]. Consequently M_n<=M_0 q^n, q=g(M_0)/M_0 (q=0 at zero). This is a strict Lyapunov certificate on the full physical intersection with the open maximum cube, including points outside the l1 octahedron. At M=alpha equal magnitudes give exactly the four critical fixed points by physical positivity. The remaining problem is trajectories with M_n>alpha for every n. This is not a global convergence theorem. Grid tests supplement the analytic inequality. No merge or deployment.
