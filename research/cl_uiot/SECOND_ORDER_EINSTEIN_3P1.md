# Second-order Einstein coefficient

For g=eta+epsilon h, inverse metric through second order is eta^-1 - epsilon h^raised + epsilon² h^raised eta h^raised. The connection coefficients obey Gamma2^r_ab=-eta^rr sum_s h_rs Gamma1^s_ab. Ricci2 contains derivatives of Gamma2 and products of Gamma1. Scalar2 includes the inverse-metric correction -h^ab Ricci1_ab. Einstein2=Ricci2-eta Scalar2/2-h Scalar1/2.

For a more general metric g=eta+epsilon h+epsilon² j, the second-order coefficient is Einstein2[h]+Einstein1[j]. The vacuum backreaction equation therefore requires solving Einstein1[j]=-Einstein2[h], not simply interpreting an unaveraged coefficient as physical energy. Second-order gauge transformations, averaging and boundary conditions are not implemented.

Six regression tests include a conformal check g=(1+2 epsilon t)eta, for which Omega=sqrt(1+2 epsilon t), and local TT waves. No merge or deployment.
