# Global fixed-point classification

Full Fourier audit enumerates every character assignment and every physical error word, using commutation with logical XXXXX, YYYYY, ZZZZZ to recover decoded Bloch signs. It compares all coefficients, not sampled inputs. Subject to this audit the closed map is x'=x(5y²+5z²-x⁴-5y²z²)/4 and cyclic permutations.

For nonzero fixed coordinates set A=x²,B=y²,C=z² in [0,1]. Subtract the x and y fixed equations: (B-A)(5+A+B-5C)=0. If both are nonzero the second factor is strictly positive, so their squared magnitudes agree. With exactly one nonzero coordinate the equation -A²=4 is impossible. With two nonzero coordinates, 5A-A²=4 implies A=1 (or 4 outside domain); two unit magnitudes and a zero third violate the physical tetrahedron. With three nonzero coordinates, 10A-6A²=4 gives A=1 or 2/3. At either magnitude the only physical sign patterns have positive product: negative product forces a probability (1-3 sqrt(A))/4<0. Thus exactly nine fixed points: center, four deterministic vertices, four symmetry-related critical points. By Pauli translation covariance their local spectra are respectively all zero, all zero, and (5/3,0,0). This is a complete fixed-point classification conditional on the full coefficient audit, not a proof of convergence of every orbit or exclusion of cycles. No hardware claims, merge or deployment.
