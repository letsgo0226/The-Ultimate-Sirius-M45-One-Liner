# FICCO: finite capture in an invariant cone

For a probability box, GIBCO encloses all physical iterates and GSCO bounds absolute Bloch coordinates. If the minimum lower bound m is positive and 5m²>=3M² for the maximum upper bound M, the whole physical subset has entered SCIO's forward-invariant ratio cone. All points entering this cone converge to one of the nine fixed points: if an orbit ever reaches a strict central or vertex amplitude region, GTREO or GREO applies. If it never does, all subsequent magnitudes straddle alpha (allowing equality). The ratio is nonincreasing and strictly decreases off the diagonal by PSGEO. Since M>=alpha and m>=sqrt(3/5)M, compactness bounds m away from zero; strict decrease forces the ratio to tend to one. Squeezing gives all magnitudes tending to alpha, with signs preserved by positive multipliers. This proves convergence, even when the exact limit label is not determined by the certificate. The finite routine does not establish that every initial state eventually enters the cone or a certified basin. No exhaustive cover run. No merge or deployment.
