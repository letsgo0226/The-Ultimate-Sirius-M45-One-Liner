# GBCO: angular ordering and a remaining global convergence problem

For a physical point outside the l1 unit ball, the coordinate product is positive and all coordinates nonzero. Pauli translation makes them positive. Write F_i=x_i m_i. Exact subtraction gives 4(m_i-m_j)=(x_j²-x_i²)(5+x_i²+x_j²-5x_k²). Thus multipliers are reverse ordered by coordinate magnitude. At most one multiplier can be nonpositive: for a coordinate that is not strictly largest, another coordinate has magnitude at least as large, and 5 b²-a⁴ >=4a², while the remaining contribution 5c²(1-b²) is nonnegative. If an output multiplier is nonpositive, the output has nonpositive product and lies in the certified l1 ball. Otherwise signs persist, and large coordinates have smaller multipliers. This is a restoring tendency, but reverse ordering alone does not prove that the ratio max/min decreases: output coordinates may cross. The implementation reports before/after ratios without asserting monotonicity. Global coverage, exclusion of cycles outside the central ball, and uniform synchronization remain open. The tests establish the algebraic factor identity on rational examples and exercise diagnostic branches; the identity itself follows by subtraction. No merge or deployment.
