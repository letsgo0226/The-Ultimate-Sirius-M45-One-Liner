# Metaplectic bridge for reduced Brinkmann dynamics

On L²(R²), use X,Y multiplication and P=-i hbar derivatives on Schwartz space, hbar>0. Weyl quantization of H=(Px²+Py²+K(u)(X²-Y²))/2 gives a real quadratic Hamiltonian. The standard metaplectic lifting theorem gives a unique continuous lift of the classical symplectic path starting at identity; its unitary representation implements the Heisenberg transformation U† Z U=F Z and solves the quadratic Schrödinger evolution with identity initial condition. This uses an established theorem, not a new proof of self-adjointness or a construction of the full propagator. The double cover matters: the endpoint F alone does not fix the sign of U.

The commutator [Z_a,Z_b]=i hbar J_ab is preserved by F J F^T=J. Code checks exact formal coefficients through finite order, independently in row convention from the previous column audit. For constant K the symmetric kick-drift-kick step has exactly symplectic rational matrices; its unitary counterpart is the product of exp(-i dt V/(2 hbar)), exp(-i dt T/hbar), exp(-i dt V/(2 hbar)), with V=K(X²-Y²)/2 and T=(Px²+Py²)/2. Each factor is unitary (potential multiplication phase or Fourier multiplier). The code implements only classical matrices, not wavefunction operators or convergence/error bounds. For time-dependent K no accuracy claim is made. This is quantization of reduced transverse mechanics on a prescribed background, not quantization of spacetime or proof of quantum gravity.
