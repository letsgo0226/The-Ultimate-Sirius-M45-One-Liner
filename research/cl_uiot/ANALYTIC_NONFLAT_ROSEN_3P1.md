# Nonflat analytic Rosen vacuum wave

Set u=t-z and ds²=dt²-dz²-cos²(epsilon u)dx²-cosh²(epsilon u)dy². On |epsilon u|<pi/2 the metric is Lorentzian and R_uu=-A''/A-B''/B=0. The transverse normalized curvature ratios are -epsilon² and +epsilon²; Riemann is nonzero when epsilon is nonzero. These ratios are not scalar polynomial curvature invariants.

The metric Taylor series are entire in epsilon u by the double-angle identities. On |epsilon u|<=r<=1/2 a bound for either component tail after order N is (2r)^m/(2 m!) divided by 1-(2r)^2/((m+1)(m+2)), where m is the first even integer greater than N. This follows from bounding successive absolute term ratios. Derivative series converge locally uniformly as well. The metric is nonsingular on this compact argument patch.

This independently chosen analytic family starts at order epsilon², not the prior solver's generic order-one TT data. It does not establish convergence of that solver. Exact polynomial checks through order six and analytical curvature and tail formulas are included. No merge or deployment.
