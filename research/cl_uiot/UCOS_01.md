# UCOS-01: bounded structural transport
FCVOC-03 passed CI run 44911812985. This separate extension transports
finite endofunctions by bijections p: T' = p T p^-1. Involutions are
mirror generators; compositions need not be involutions. Finite orbit
enumeration deduplicates exact tables and caps states, domain and generators.
In general relational structures transport each relation along the
bijection, preserving first-order satisfaction when all symbols are
transported consistently. Order reversal, logical duality, and physical
parity require separate hypotheses; not all are symmetries of a fixed
theory (e.g. weak-interaction parity violation).
A single involution generates at most two representations. Multiple
generators or increasing domain sizes can motivate unbounded mathematical
families, not automatic unbounded execution. Finite domain orbits are
finite (at most n!); no new semantic knowledge follows merely by relabeling.
No network, self-replication, executable payloads, or deployment.
This implementation handles endofunctions only; relational transport and
FCVOC cross-verification/Lean proof remain future work.
