# Formal critical manifold recursion

Use coordinates u=vX, v=vY, vZ=-u-v and radial offset r=total error-pc. Inputs are wc+(-H,H/3+u,H/3+v,H/3-u-v). Compute outputs r',u',v' exactly as bivariate truncated polynomials over Q(sqrt(6)). The defect E(H)=r'-H(u',v'). Since the radial derivative is 5/3 and transverse derivative vanishes, adding any homogeneous correction C_k of degree k>=2 changes the degree-k defect by exactly (5/3) C_k, leaving all lower degrees unchanged. Therefore recursively set C_k=-3 E_k/5. This uniquely determines each finite jet with zero constant and linear terms. All retained defect coefficients are independently recomputed at the end. The smooth local stable manifold has these jets by invariance and uniqueness. The algorithm is finite for each requested order, not a proof of convergence of the infinite Taylor series or of global basin classification. Tests cover through order five; larger orders may be computationally expensive. No hardware validation, merge or deployment.
