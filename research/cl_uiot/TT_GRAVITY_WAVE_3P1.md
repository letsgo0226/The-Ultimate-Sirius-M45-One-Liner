# Linearized TT plane waves

For propagation in the positive z direction, choose h_11=f(t-z), h_22=-f(t-z), h_12=h_21=g(t-z), and all other components zero. This satisfies trace zero, harmonic gauge and the vacuum linearized Einstein equation. The two prescribed polarization profiles are independent. The linearized curvature R_0i0j=-1/2 partial_t² h_ij in TT gauge is invariant under infinitesimal gauge changes on flat background. Nonzero tidal curvature distinguishes these examples from pure gauge. Constant or affine profiles need not have nonzero curvature.

The exact rational polynomial examples are local, not globally bounded radiative solutions. Tests do not establish general gauge fixing, a complete degree-of-freedom classification, nonlinear gravity or quantum gravity. No deployment or merge.
