# Universal Pauli renormalization polynomial

Their sum is (pI+pX+pY+pZ)^5, so the simplex is invariant. In coordinates (pX,pY,pZ), pI=1-pX-pY-pZ, differentiate each monomial exactly using partial_j-partial_I. Identity and uniform points have zero tangent Jacobian (identity by single-error correction; uniform by cancellation of first-order biases, equivalently nontrivial decoded logical Pauli observables have no weight-one Fourier terms). Thus both are locally attracting in the full simplex. The radial derivative on the symmetric slice agrees with ELCR.
