# USRCO-01: exact projective coordinate atlas
UCOS-01 passed CI 44912598515. Model the rational projective line by
nonzero homogeneous pairs [u:v] modulo nonzero rational scaling.
Charts x=u/v and y=v/u overlap with xy=1. Reflection [u:v]->[-u:v]
is an involution. The evolution [u:v]->[u:v-u] has determinant one,
hence is globally invertible. In x it is x/(1-x), undefined at x=1
as a real-valued expression; in y it is y-1. The projective extension
passes through [1:0] without loss of the projective state.
This is a discrete rational model of chart failure, not a Schwarzschild
metric or removal of a curvature singularity. No claim about arbitrary
programs or halting. Stirling bounds and zeta completion are separate
future layers. Tests enumerate 288 nonzero pairs in [-8,8]^2, not
all rational points. Pure functions; tests bounded, no autonomous
execution, deployment or replication. Lean formalization pending.
