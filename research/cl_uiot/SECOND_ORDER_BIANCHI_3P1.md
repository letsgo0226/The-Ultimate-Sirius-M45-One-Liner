# Second-order contracted Bianchi identity

For g=eta+epsilon h the mixed Einstein coefficient at order two is M2^a_b=eta^aa G2_ab-h^as G1_sb. Expanding nabla_a G^a_b=0 gives partial_a M2^a_b + Gamma1^a_as eta^ss G1_sb - Gamma1^s_ab eta^aa G1_as=0 (sum repeated indices).

When G1=0 all correction terms vanish and partial^a G2_ab=0. This is necessary for solving G1[j]=-G2[h]. Off shell the ordinary divergence need not vanish. The tests include a conformal off-shell example and mixed-component polynomial data. No existence theorem, nonlinear evolution or boundary-value solver is claimed. No merge or deployment.
