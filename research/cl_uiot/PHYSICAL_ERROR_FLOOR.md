# Physical error floor in an ideal Pauli channel model

Use independent depolarizing physical noise with 0<p<1 and ideal five-qubit syndrome recovery. A weight-two witness has nontrivial logical residual; its exact probability is (p/3)^2(1-p)^3. The implementation enumerates all 1024 physical labels and classifies residuals into stabilizer cosets I, XXXXX, YYYYY, ZZZZZ (phases ignored). Recursive independent blocks yield a full four-component logical Pauli distribution, without assuming depolarizing symmetry. Each logical coset has a representative at every concatenation depth: recursively encode the corresponding logical Pauli representative, with identity elsewhere as needed. All leaf patterns have probability at least min(1-p,p/3)^(5^n), so each logical class has that positive lower bound. At fixed n and fresh independent noise each cycle, with ideal channel reset and first nonidentity logical Pauli defined as failure, the conditional hazard is constant and positive; the FRNT survival bound applies. This is a trajectory-error event, not necessarily experimentally observable state damage or final-time failure; later errors may cancel. No claim for arbitrary correlated noise, hardware, noisy recovery or all fixed-resource systems. Exact rational enumeration is expensive at large depths. No merge or deployment.
