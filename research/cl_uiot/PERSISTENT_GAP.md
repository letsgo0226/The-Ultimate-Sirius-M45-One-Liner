# PSGEO: scale-dependent synchronization cone

Let M and m denote maximum and minimum absolute Bloch coordinates. On the physical region m>0, 5m²>=3M⁴, all multipliers are positive: P_i>=5m² >=3M⁴ > a_i⁴, since b²+c²-b²c²>=m². For any unequal pair a>b, multiplier ordering is strictly reverse, as 4(A_b-A_a)=(a²-b²)(5+a²+b²-5c²)>0. Thus F_a/F_b<a/b. The crossing identity 4(a² A_a-b² A_b)=(a²-b²)(5c²-a⁴-a²b²-b⁴)>0 holds because a>b makes the sum strictly smaller than 3M⁴. Hence F_a/F_b>b/a. Equal input pairs remain equal. Consequently max/min strictly decreases except on the diagonal. This extends UASO from its fixed lower magnitude threshold to a scale-dependent cone.

For a permanently cross-critical orbit eventually staying in this cone, M>=alpha and m²>=3M⁴/5>=4/15. Compactness and continuity then imply max/min tends to 1: otherwise a subsequence converges to a nondiagonal point with strict ratio decrease, contradicting convergence of the monotone ratio. Since m<alpha<M, both converge to alpha. Positive multipliers preserve signs and physical positivity requires positive sign product outside the central octahedron. Hence the orbit converges to the corresponding saddle. Any remaining nonconvergent permanently cross-critical orbit must violate 5m²>=3M⁴ infinitely often. No proof of eventual cone entry or invariance is asserted. Tests check finite inequalities only. No merge or deployment.
