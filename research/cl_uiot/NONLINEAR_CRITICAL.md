# Nonlinear critical manifold: quadratic jet

Use total error offset r=pX+pY+pZ-pc and transverse v with vI=0 and vX+vY+vZ=0. At the critical point the linear map is r'=5r/3, v'=0. Write the local stable graph r=H(v), H(0)=DH(0)=0. Taylor expansion gives r'=5H_2(v)/3+B(v)+O(||v||^3), where B(v)=sum of the nonidentity output coefficients of t^2 in R(wc+tv). Since v'=O(||v||²), H(v')=O(||v||^4). Invariance therefore implies H_2(v)=-3B(v)/5. This is a quadratic graph formula, not a full convergent graph algorithm. The code computes exact directional degree-five jets at rational simplex points and Hessian bilinear actions by polarization. The critical point is irrational; the formula at wc is analytical, while rational evaluations are polynomial extensions, not critical-point numerical certificates. No global basin classification, hardware validation, merge or deployment.
