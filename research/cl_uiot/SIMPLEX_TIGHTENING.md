For B intersect simplex, fixing coordinate i to t is feasible iff t lies in its original interval and 1-t lies between the sum of the other lower bounds and sum of the other upper bounds. Every intermediate sum is attained by independent intervals. Thus the new bounds are exact coordinate extrema, preserve the physical intersection, and are idempotent. This is not a representation of all correlations. Applying tightening before and after outward interval iteration is sound because every physical image remains on the simplex.
