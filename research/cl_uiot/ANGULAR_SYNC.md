# UASO regional angular synchronization

The identity 4(a² M_a-b² M_b)=(a²-b²)(5c²-a⁴-a²b²-b⁴) gives a'/b'>b/a: the second factor is strictly positive since a>b implies a⁴+a²b²+b⁴<3. Therefore even if outputs cross, absolute log ratio strictly decreases. Equal inputs remain equal. Taking the maximum over pairs shows spread max/min strictly decreases unless already one. On any orbit staying in this compact region forever, the spread converges to one: otherwise compactness supplies a subsequential limit with spread >1 and continuity forces a strict decrement, contradicting convergence of the spread. This argument does not establish forward invariance of the region, eventual entry, or synchronization of every orbit outside the central octahedron.
