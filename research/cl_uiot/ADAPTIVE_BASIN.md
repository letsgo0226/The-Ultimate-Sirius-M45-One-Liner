# ABCO: adaptive coverage with finite resource bounds

Breadth-first subdivision starts with a physical intersection of a rational probability box and the simplex. For each popped box, GIBCO attempts a finite orbit certificate. Successful boxes become labelled leaves. Otherwise bisect the widest coordinate unless at the depth limit or a singleton. When the box-processing budget ends, every queued box becomes an unresolved leaf. Thus by induction the physical root equals the union of physical leaf intersections; boundaries may overlap. Every labelled leaf is sound by GIBCO. The budget bounds the number of certificate calls, not wall time or memory; each call is bounded by cap and rounding precision. Replay verification recomputes the deterministic search and compares the entire record, not an independent implementation. Counts are counts of boxes, not probabilities or simplex volume. No full-scale cover was executed in this change. Unresolved leaves need not equal stable manifolds; proving that requires further dynamics. No merge or deployment.
