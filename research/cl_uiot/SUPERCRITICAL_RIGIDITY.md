# SORO: exclusion of a near-critical weak-coordinate band

Let M=max absolute coordinate, with sqrt(2/3)<=M<=5/6 and min absolute coordinate <=2/3. Write the output magnitude as |a(P-a^4)|/4 where P=5(b²+c²-b²c²), K=10M²-5M⁴. Negative signed outputs are <=1/4. Positive outputs with a<=1/2 are <=5/8 since K<=5. If a is a minimum coordinate, the gap from g(M) is at least [10 M²(1-M²)(M-a)]/4 by the derivative bound for t(K-t^4). Here M-a>=4/5-2/3=2/15. Otherwise one of b,c is a minimum coordinate <=2/3. Then K-P>=5(1-M²)(M²-4/9)>=5(11/36)(2/9). For a>1/2 the output gap is at least this quantity divided by eight. Since g is increasing, replacing M by 5/6 in g gives the exact rational maximum of the four bounds implemented in ceiling(). Its square is strictly less than 2/3. Thus every physical point in this band enters the GTREO central basin in one step. Any perpetually strictly supercritical orbit visiting M<=5/6 must therefore have every absolute coordinate >2/3 at that visit. This does not imply the UASO condition min squared magnitude >=3/5, nor does it cover M>5/6. Global synchronization remains open. Grid tests supplement the analytic proof. No merge or deployment.
