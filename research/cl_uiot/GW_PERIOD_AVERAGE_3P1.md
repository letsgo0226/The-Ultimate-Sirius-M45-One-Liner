# Exact periodic averaging of TT wave energy

For finite real Fourier profiles in phase u=w(t-z), use nonnegative integer harmonics with rational cosine/sine coefficients. w is a common nonzero angular frequency; the code also permits w=0 algebraically, not as a finite-period wave. The average over a full period projects onto the constant Fourier coefficient. Orthogonality yields <f'g'> = sum_{n>=1} n²(a_n c_n+b_n d_n)/2 for phase derivatives. The averaged TT kernel is K_ab = w² sum_{n>=1} n²(a_plus²+b_plus²+a_cross²+b_cross²) k_a k_b, k=(1,0,0,-1).

Multiplication by 1/(32 pi G_N) gives the leading Isaacson expression in its appropriate weak-field shortwave regime. This exact period average is not a general curved-background spacetime average or proof of second-order backreaction. Inputs must be valid nonnegative integer harmonic indices; this prototype does not validate that contract. No merge or deployment.
