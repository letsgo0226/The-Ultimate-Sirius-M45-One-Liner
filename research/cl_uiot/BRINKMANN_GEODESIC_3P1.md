# Brinkmann geodesic completeness and evolution

For the smooth polynomial K metric du dV-dX²-dY²+K(u)(X²-Y²)du², the affinely parametrized geodesic has u''=0. When u'=a is nonzero, use u as parameter: X_uu=-K X, Y_uu=K Y. Continuous linear ODE coefficients guarantee unique solutions on every finite interval, forward and backward. The fundamental maps compose by uniqueness, and their determinants are 1 by the zero trace of the first-order system (equivalently the Wronskian). The conserved norm c gives dV/dlambda=(c+X_dot²+Y_dot²-H a²)/a, continuous on every finite interval; integration yields V and the remaining geodesic equation follows by norm conservation and the other equations. When a=0 the transverse coordinates and V are affine in lambda directly from the geodesic equations. Hence this smooth metric on R^4 is geodesically complete, for all causal types. Completeness does not imply bounded coordinates at infinite parameter or general spacetime completeness.

The implementation computes exact local Taylor jets for polynomial K around rational s and checks Wronskian coefficients through finite order. It is not a global numerical integrator or certified finite-time transition matrix evaluator. The completeness and composition results follow from the analytical ODE argument, not the finite tests.
