# Quantum entropy invariant

For finite-dimensional density matrices, unitary conjugation preserves the spectrum and hence von Neumann entropy S=-Tr(rho log2 rho), including zero eigenvalues with 0 log 0=0. It cannot purify a mixed state. The code demonstrates this with real symmetric trace-one positive semidefinite 2x2 matrices and rational orthogonal rotations (a subset of unitaries). Trace, determinant and purity are exact rational invariants; numerical entropy in bits uses floating-point logarithms, not a certified error bound. A pure superposition with equal diagonal probabilities has von Neumann entropy zero but computational-basis measurement Shannon entropy one bit. Dephasing changes the state, unlike closed unitary evolution. Thermodynamic entropy requires separate physical assumptions. This toy finite-dimensional model is not a discretization of the infinite-dimensional Brinkmann propagator or proof of hardware-independent persistence. No merge or deployment.
