# PCEGCO: matched physical interval comparison
Run baseline FICCO and PSITO-tightened FICCO on identical nodes. Stop splitting when either succeeds; retain pending nodes as unknown in both columns. The combined leaves cover the root. Monotonicity of the nonnegative-coefficient probability interval map, outward rounding and tightening ensures tightened enclosures are contained in baseline enclosures at equal times. Every baseline certificate predicate is preserved under box inclusion (though the label or first entry time can differ). Hence tightened certification cannot lose a baseline success. The experiment uses 255 paired node evaluations, depth 8, cap 1, digits 12. This is matched-node certification efficiency, not equal CPU time or two independent adaptive covers. No volume interpretation. CI prints measured counts; no result presumed. No merge or deployment.
