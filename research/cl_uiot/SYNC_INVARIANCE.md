# SCIO: invariant synchronization subregions

Write R=M/m for positive magnitudes. PSGEO proves R_next<=R in K, where K is equivalent to R*M<=sqrt(5/3). GTREO gives M_next<=g(M)<=M for M<=alpha=sqrt(2/3). Hence K intersect {M<=alpha} is forward invariant: R_next*M_next<=R*M. The second subset {m>0, R<=sqrt(5/3)} is contained in K because M<=1, and the ratio inequality proves its forward invariance without a radial assumption. These two subsets may overlap. Every permanently cross-critical orbit entering the second subset converges to a saddle by PSGEO. This does not prove invariance of all K, or eventual entry into either subset. In particular the remaining region M>alpha and sqrt(5/3)<R<=sqrt(5/3)/M is not covered. Rational tests check finite-step invariance; no full global search, merge or deployment.
