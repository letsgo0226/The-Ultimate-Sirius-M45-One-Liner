# Five-qubit perfect stabilizer code

Generators XZZXI, IXZZX, XIXZZ, ZXIXZ commute and are independent, defining a two-dimensional common +1 eigenspace P. All 16 identity/single-qubit Pauli errors have distinct four-bit syndromes. For distinct errors E_a,E_b the product anticommutes with a generator, so P E_a† E_b P=0; for identical errors it equals P. By Knill-Laflamme this also corrects arbitrary single-qubit channels by linear span. Syndrome recovery applies the matching Pauli. The quantum Hamming bound is saturated: 2*(1+3*5)=32. Exhaustive phase-free Pauli enumeration verifies distance three, ignoring global phases which do not affect syndromes or Pauli conjugation channels. Independence and commutation ensure the signed generators do not generate -I. Code checks algebraic syndromes and residual labels, not a statevector circuit or noisy syndrome extraction. Two-qubit errors can yield logical errors. Ideal operations assumed; no claim of indefinite storage or hardware independence. No merge or deployment.
