# M45-ST/41 — Fictional Consciousness Fragment (G-2112)

A small **fictional narrative protocol** for an unofficial Doraemon side-story. It encodes a labelled 2112→2026 memory-text fragment, validates its SHA-256 digest and expected narrative semantics, and generates a Traditional Chinese story response. This is **not** a consciousness upload, mind copying, retrocausal messaging or a mechanism for physical effects.

The story keeps Doraemon's 2112 fictional birth intact. The 2026 date is the narrative arrival of a story fragment, not a second literal birth.

```sh
python3 -m unittest discover -s m45-st41 -p 'test_*.py'
python3 m45-st41/fictional_bridge.py --out fragment.json
```

This milestone intentionally implements only the fictional fragment concept. It does not claim an exhaustive GitHub knowledge import, a physical actuator, morphological-field mechanism, or indefinite execution. SHA-256 is an integrity check, not proof of provenance or identity.
